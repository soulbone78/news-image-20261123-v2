# news-image-20261123 — "The World in One Day — 23 November 2026"

A one-page newspaper front page summing up Monday 23 November
2026. `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) paints the
page in code and writes a 1024x1024 PNG to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (or `/output` when that variable
is unset).

## Stories on the page

1. **Lead — the Gulf war:** Iranian missiles hit a civilian plant
   in Sitra, Bahrain (2 hurt, plant ablaze); two tankers are hit
   near the Strait of Hormuz; Saudi jets down a Houthi missile
   aimed at Saudi Arabia, and the US denies an Iranian claim it
   hit a US jet.
2. **COP31 collapses** in Antalya: the EU rejects a final text
   with no mention of fossil fuels.
3. **Kyiv region:** a Russian drone smashes a five-storey flat
   block; 14 feared trapped, nine people safe.
4. **NATO meets** in Brussels after Russian missiles and a drone
   cross into Poland.
5. **Nepal PM Balendra Shah** meets PM Narendra Modi in New Delhi
   on a state visit.
6. **Sport:** India declare on 720 for 9 in the Wellington Test,
   a record total.
7. **Also today:** a factory fire in Indonesia (7 dead, 50
   unaccounted for); Seoul's education minister quits in an AI
   exam row; Lisbon marches on the cost of life.

## How it is drawn

Flat vector art only — no photos. Everything is painted on a
canvas twice the final size and shrunk by 2x2 averaging; words
come from a built-in 5x7 dot face. No network, no clock, no
randomness: two runs write byte-identical PNGs. Body copy avoids
a lowercase "g", which the small face renders like a "9".
